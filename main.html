Manali


Manali is a beautiful hill station in Himachal Pradesh, known for its snow-covered mountains, scenic valleys, and pleasant climate. It is a perfect destination for nature lovers and adventure enthusiasts.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Solang Valley – famous for adventure sports
  2. Rohtang Pass – known for its snow views
  3. Hadimba Temple – surrounded by cedar forests
  4. Old Manali – popular for its cafes and culture
  5. Vashisht Hot Springs – relaxing natural hot water

Must-Try Foods

  • Siddu – a traditional Himachali dish
  • Thukpa – a popular noodle soup
  • Chana Madra – rich yogurt-based curry
  • Momos – famous street food
  • Apple desserts – made from local apples
