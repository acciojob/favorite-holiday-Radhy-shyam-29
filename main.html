Manali


Manali is a beautiful hill station located in Himachal Pradesh, known for its scenic mountains, pleasant weather, and adventure activities. It is a perfect destination for nature lovers and travelers seeking peace and excitement.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Solang Valley – Famous for snow sports and paragliding.
  2. Rohtang Pass – A breathtaking high-altitude mountain pass.
  3. Hadimba Temple – An ancient wooden temple surrounded by cedar forests.
  4. Old Manali – Known for cafes, culture, and scenic views.
  5. Vashisht Hot Springs – Natural hot water springs with spiritual significance.

Must-Try Foods

  • Siddu – A traditional steamed Himachali bread.
  • Thukpa – A warm and comforting noodle soup.
  • Chana Madra – A rich yogurt-based curry.
  • Babru – A local stuffed bread dish.
  • Mittha – A sweet Himachali dessert.
